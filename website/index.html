Welcome to OpenTube.

A high-performance YouTube and YouTube Music client for Android with an on-device recommendation engine. No accounts needed, no telemetry, no surveillance.

Download Latest APK Star on GitHub

Engineered for Audio and Video Freedom

Everything you love about YouTube and YouTube Music without the friction.

🧠

On-Device Intelligence

Smart recommendations that run entirely on your phone. Discovers what you like without ever sending your watch history to a remote server.

🎧

Background & Picture-in-Picture

Listen to audio with your screen turned off or keep watching videos in floating PiP mode while multitasking in other apps.

🚫

SponsorBlock & DeArrow

Automatically skips sponsored segments, intros, and self-promotion. Replaces clickbait thumbnails and titles with community alternatives.

🎵

Dedicated Music Experience

Full-fledged music player with synchronized real-time lyrics, queue management, album artwork, and audio visualizers.

📥

Offline Media Downloads

Download full-resolution video and high-bitrate audio directly to your device storage with VP9, AV1, and standard format support.

🔒

Absolute Privacy

No Google Account required. Zero advertising trackers, no telemetry SDKs, and complete freedom to export or delete your local data anytime.
